ClipFactory

A private desktop tool that turns long videos into vertical Shorts and schedules them on its owner's own YouTube channels.

ClipFactory uses YouTube API Services

ClipFactory connects to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API. It is used only by its owner, Henrique de Cesare Pacheco Frainer, to publish Shorts on three YouTube channels that the owner owns and manages. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

By using ClipFactory you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also read our Privacy Policy, our Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

Edits

Finds the best moments of a long video, reframes them to 9:16 and adds captions, all on the owner's computer.

Schedules

Uploads each Short to the owner's channel as private with a scheduled publish time, every few hours per channel.

Checks

Reads the status of its own uploads to confirm each Short went public at the right time.
