Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

ClipFactory ("the tool") is a private desktop application operated by Henrique de Cesare Pacheco Frainer, Av. Victor Barreto, 2138, apt 1202, Canoas, RS, Brasil ("we"). It is used only by its operator to manage YouTube channels the operator owns. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

ClipFactory uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipFactory you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Data we access

  • Google account authorization (OAuth 2.0): an access token and a refresh token for each YouTube channel that the operator connects, with the scopes needed to upload videos and manage the channel's own videos.
  • Channel information: the channel ID, title and profile picture, used to confirm that each authorization belongs to the right channel and to show the channel in the tool's local dashboard.
  • Video information of the channel's own uploads: video IDs, privacy status and scheduled publish time, used to schedule Shorts and to confirm they were published.

ClipFactory does not access data of any other YouTube user, does not read comments, subscribers or analytics of other channels, and does not collect personal data from viewers.

How we use it

The data is used only to upload the operator's own Shorts, set their title, description, tags and publish time, and check that they were published. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or combined with other data.

Storage and security

Tokens and the IDs of the tool's own uploads are stored only on the operator's computer, in a local folder that is not synced or shared. No YouTube data is stored on any server. Data is transmitted to Google only over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens: renewed by Google's token refresh when used; deleted immediately when a channel is disconnected in the tool, and within 7 days after access is revoked at Google (the tool checks every channel once a day).
  • Channel ID, title and profile picture: refreshed every 24 hours; deleted immediately on disconnect and within 7 days of a revocation.
  • Status of the tool's own uploads (privacy status, scheduled time): read when needed until the video is public; not stored.
  • Video IDs of the tool's own uploads: deleted 30 days after the video goes public, or immediately when the channel is disconnected (IDs of videos not yet public are kept only until they go public, so a video is never uploaded twice).
  • Publish times of the channel's recent videos: read when scheduling and kept only in memory.
  • Logs and activity records that mention video IDs: deleted after 30 days; local database backups after 14 days.
  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • To request deletion of any data, write to henriquefrainer@gmail.com; it is deleted within 7 days.

Cookies

This website and the tool do not use cookies or tracking technologies, and do not place any on viewers' devices.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions: henriquefrainer@gmail.com.
